## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for how people may use [Organization Name] information, systems, devices, and networks. As an IT company, [Organization Name] is trusted with its own data and with access to customer systems and data. This policy protects that trust by making clear what is allowed, what is not, and what happens when the rules are broken.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees, contractors, interns, temporary staff, and third parties who access [Organization Name] information or systems.
- All company-owned or company-managed assets, including laptops, mobile devices, servers, cloud services, networks, software, accounts, and data.
- Personal devices used to access company or customer information.
- Customer systems and data that workforce members access while delivering services.
- Use of these assets at any location, including the office, home, customer sites, and public places.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Information Asset | Any information, system, device, application, or account owned, managed, or entrusted to [Organization Name]. |
| Customer Data | Any information belonging to a customer that [Organization Name] stores, processes, or can access while providing services. |
| Workforce Member | Any employee, contractor, intern, or third party covered by this policy. |
| Privileged Access | Administrator or elevated rights that allow a user to change system settings, manage other accounts, or bypass normal restrictions. |
| Personal Device | A device not owned by [Organization Name] that is used to access company or customer information. |
| Generative AI Tool | A service that creates text, code, images, or other content from user prompts. |
| Incidental Personal Use | Brief, occasional personal use of company assets that does not interfere with work or violate this policy. |

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Executive Leadership | Approves this policy and supports its enforcement. |
| Policy Owner (CISO or equivalent) | Maintains and reviews this policy. Approves exceptions. Reports violations and trends to leadership. |
| IT and Security Team | Implements technical controls that support this policy, such as endpoint protection and web filtering. Investigates reported violations. |
| People Managers | Make sure their team members read, acknowledge, and follow this policy. Report suspected violations. |
| Human Resources | Collects signed acknowledgments. Applies the disciplinary process when violations occur. Coordinates return of assets when a workforce member leaves or changes roles. |
| All Workforce Members | Read, acknowledge, and follow this policy. Report suspected security events and policy violations. |

## 5. Policy Statements

### 5.1 General Use and Ownership

- **AUP-01:** Information assets are provided for business purposes. Incidental personal use is allowed only if it is brief, lawful, does not interfere with work, and follows this policy.
- **AUP-02:** Workforce members must use information assets only in ways they are authorized to use them.
- **AUP-03:** Workforce members must read and sign the acknowledgment in Appendix B before receiving access, and again after each major revision of this policy.

### 5.2 Accounts and Authentication

- **AUP-04:** Each workforce member must use only the accounts assigned to them. Accounts must never be shared.
- **AUP-05:** Passwords, passphrases, tokens, and other authentication information must be kept confidential and must never be written down in unsecured locations, sent by email or chat, or stored in plain text.
- **AUP-06:** Multi-factor authentication must be used wherever [Organization Name] has enabled it.
- **AUP-07:** Workforce members must immediately report any suspected compromise of their account or authentication information.

### 5.3 Endpoint Devices and Software

- **AUP-08:** Company devices must keep the security settings and protection software installed by IT. Workforce members must not disable, bypass, or uninstall security tools such as anti-malware software.
- **AUP-09:** Only software approved by IT may be installed on company devices.
- **AUP-10:** Workforce members must lock their screen whenever they step away from their device.
- **AUP-11:** Removable storage media, such as USB drives, may be used only if approved by IT, and must be protected according to the classification of the information stored on them.

### 5.4 Email, Internet, and Communications

- **AUP-12:** Company email, chat, and collaboration tools must be used professionally and must not be used to send harassing, discriminatory, or offensive content.
- **AUP-13:** Workforce members must be cautious with unexpected links and attachments and must report suspected phishing messages to [reporting channel].
- **AUP-14:** Workforce members must not attempt to bypass web filtering or other network security controls.
- **AUP-15:** Workforce members must not post confidential company or customer information on social media or public forums, and must not speak on behalf of [Organization Name] unless authorized.

### 5.5 Handling Company and Customer Information

- **AUP-16:** Information must be handled according to its classification level under the [Organization Name] classification scheme.
- **AUP-17:** Company and customer information must be shared only through approved, secure methods and only with people authorized to receive it.
- **AUP-18:** Customer data must be accessed only when needed to deliver contracted services and only within the scope approved by the customer.
- **AUP-19:** Company and customer information must not be copied to personal email, personal cloud storage, or unapproved services.

### 5.6 Remote Work and Off-Premises Use

- **AUP-20:** When working outside the office, workforce members must protect devices and information from theft, loss, and viewing by others.
- **AUP-21:** Company resources must be accessed from outside the office only through approved secure connections, such as the company VPN where required.
- **AUP-22:** Devices must never be left unattended in public places or visible in unattended vehicles.

### 5.7 Personal Devices

- **AUP-23:** Personal devices may access company information only if approved by IT and only if they meet the security requirements set by IT.
- **AUP-24:** Company and customer information on a personal device may be removed by [Organization Name] when the device is lost, stolen, or no longer used for work.

### 5.8 Privileged Access

- **AUP-25:** Privileged access must be used only for the specific tasks that require it. Workforce members with privileged access must use a separate standard account for everyday work such as email and web browsing.
- **AUP-26:** Privileged access must never be used to view information, change settings, or access systems beyond what a task requires.

### 5.9 Generative AI Tools

- **AUP-27:** Only generative AI tools approved by [Organization Name] may be used for work.
- **AUP-28:** Confidential company information, customer data, source code, and authentication information must not be entered into any generative AI tool unless IT has approved that tool for that type of information.
- **AUP-29:** Workforce members are responsible for reviewing AI-generated content, including code, for accuracy and security before using it.

### 5.10 Clear Desk and Clear Screen

- **AUP-30:** Confidential printed material must be removed from desks, printers, and meeting rooms when not in use and stored or disposed of securely.
- **AUP-31:** Screens displaying confidential information must be positioned or protected so that unauthorized people cannot view them.

### 5.11 Reporting Security Events

- **AUP-32:** Workforce members must report suspected security events, lost or stolen devices, and policy violations to [reporting channel] as soon as they notice them. Reports made in good faith will not result in disciplinary action.

### 5.12 Return and Disposal of Assets

- **AUP-33:** Workforce members must return all company assets, including devices, access badges, tokens, and information, when they leave [Organization Name] or when their role no longer requires them.
- **AUP-34:** Company devices and storage media must be disposed of or reused only through IT so that data is securely removed first.

## 6. Prohibited Activities

The following are not allowed under any circumstances unless specifically authorized in writing by the Policy Owner for a legitimate business purpose, such as approved security testing:

| # | Prohibited Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 | Accessing, or attempting to access, systems, accounts, or data without authorization, including customer systems outside the approved scope. |
| 2 | Scanning, probing, or testing the security of any network or system. |
| 3 | Installing or running hacking tools, password crackers, or network sniffers. |
| 4 | Introducing malware or intentionally disrupting any system or service. |
| 5 | Sharing accounts or authentication information. |
| 6 | Disabling or bypassing security controls. |
| 7 | Using company assets for illegal activity, harassment, or accessing sexually explicit or hateful material. |
| 8 | Using company assets for personal business ventures, cryptocurrency mining, or other personal financial gain. |
| 9 | Installing unlicensed software or distributing copyrighted material without permission. |
| 10 | Sending company or customer information to personal accounts or unapproved services. |

## 7. Monitoring

To the extent permitted by applicable law, [Organization Name] may monitor the use of its information assets, including network traffic, email, and device activity, to protect its systems, its customers, and its workforce. Workforce members should not expect personal privacy when using company assets. Monitoring is carried out only by authorized personnel and only for legitimate business and security purposes.

## 8. Compliance and Enforcement

- Compliance with this policy may be monitored and audited.
- Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract, in accordance with the [Organization Name] disciplinary process.
- Access may be suspended during an investigation.
- Where a violation may involve illegal activity, [Organization Name] may refer the matter to the appropriate authorities after consulting Legal.

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a business justification and any compensating controls, be approved by the Policy Owner, and have a documented expiration date.

## 10. Policy Review

This policy must be reviewed at least annually, and after any significant security incident or major change in technology, business operations, or legal requirements.
